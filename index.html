Lane Siege

A tower defence and lane battle game for iPhone. Build a maze of towers to hold your lane, send units to break your rival's, and pull the Heartstone to your side.

Support

Questions, problems or a lost account? E-mail lanesiege.support@gmail.com and include your Player ID (in the game under Settings). To delete your account and all its data, use Settings › Delete account in the game, or e-mail us.
