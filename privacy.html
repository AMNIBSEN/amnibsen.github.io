Lane Siege privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

Lane Siege has no analytics and only shows an ad when you choose to watch one for a reward. It keeps only what it needs to save your game, sell you the items you choose to buy, find you an opponent and show you the ads you ask for. Your data is never sold.

What the game keeps

  • The name you choose and your avatar, frame and medal.
  • Your game progress: levels, cards, gear, coins, gems, trophies, quests and settings.
  • Your Game Center player ID, from Apple, so you can sign in and your progress is saved to your iCloud as a Game Center saved game.
  • Your purchases: what you bought and when. RevenueCat checks each App Store receipt with Apple and gets an anonymous app ID, never your name, e-mail or payment card.

Ads

When you choose to watch an ad for a reward, Google AdMob shows it. AdMob receives your device type, IP address and how you interact with the ad, and uses your advertising ID only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks. In the EU and UK, Google asks for your consent first. You can change your ad choices any time in the game under Settings › Ad choices, or in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's own privacy policy covers what AdMob does: policies.google.com/privacy.

What the game never collects

No usage statistics or analytics, no precise location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone. Your data is never sold.

Who can see it

  • Other players, only if you allow live matches: your name, level, trophies, hero and avatar, while you are matched with them.
  • Services that run the game: Apple (Game Center, iCloud and the App Store), RevenueCat (purchases) and Google AdMob (the ads you choose to watch). They handle your data only to provide those services.

Your choices

  • Change your privacy choices any time in the game under Settings › Privacy.
  • Delete your saved game in your iPhone's Settings › Game Center, or delete the app.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, use the Support link on Lane Siege's App Store page.

Children

Lane Siege collects nothing beyond what is listed here from anyone, whatever their age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the game shows you its privacy screen again before you play, and this page shows the new date.
